What is Power Platform?

Microsoft Power Platform is a suite of tools including Power BI, Power Apps, Power Automate, and Power Virtual Agents. It enables businesses to create custom solutions with minimal coding.

Core Components

Power BI

Turn data into interactive dashboards and business insights for smarter decision-making.

Power Apps

Build custom business applications quickly without heavy coding experience.

Power Automate

Automate repetitive tasks and workflows across multiple systems and services.

Power Virtual Agents

Create AI-powered chatbots to improve customer engagement and support.
